# (let's try) Positioning — UAE Real Estate Teams

## Who It Is For (ICP)
- Brokerage owners and sales managers in UAE real estate.
- Teams sitting on old CRM lists, stale Meta leads, and WhatsApp follow-up backlogs.
- Agencies that need more conversations without hiring more callers.

## The Pain
- Old leads are not dead; they are just unfollowed.
- Agents lose deals because outreach is inconsistent, late, or manual.
- Managers lack clear visibility on who was called, what was said, and what to do next.

## The Promise
- Reactivate old leads at scale with automated outbound calling.
- Launch a campaign in minutes, not weeks.
- Convert cold lists into qualified conversations while your team focuses on closings.

## The Proof
- Upload leads, run campaigns, and track outcomes in one dashboard.
- See calls placed, answered, failed, voicemail, callbacks, and transcripts.
- Use credits + assigned numbers to scale predictably.

## Why It Is Different
- Two voice lanes: ElevenLabs (simple SaaS) and self-hosted Sesame CSM (high-control tier).
- Conversation-first onboarding with defaults that get teams to first call fast.
- Built-in trust controls: automated-call disclosure, opt-out handling, quiet-hours guardrails.

## Launch Message
"Your old leads are still valuable. (let's try) helps your team call them now, qualify intent faster, and recover pipeline revenue at scale."
